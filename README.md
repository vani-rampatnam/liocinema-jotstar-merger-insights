# 📊 Liocinema-Jotstar Merger Insights

A Power BI project that analyzes the business performance of two leading OTT platforms, **Liocinema** and **Jotstar**, to evaluate the feasibility and strategy of a potential merger. The insights derived help support strategic decisions around subscriber retention, monetization, and marketing alignment.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

This analysis simulates a real-world business scenario where two OTT giants consider merging their operations. The Power BI dashboard explores:

- Retention of existing subscribers  
- Plan upgrade & downgrade trends  
- Localized vs global marketing effectiveness  
- Brand ambassador selection through demographic analysis  

It aims to empower stakeholders with **data-driven insights** for a stronger post-merger market position.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs Tracked

- Total Content  
- Jotstar & Liocinema Content Comparison  
- New Subscriptions  
- Active vs Inactive Users  
- Upgrade/Downgrade Patterns  
- Total Revenue (platform-wise)  
- Paid vs Free Users  

---

## 📊 Visuals & Dashboards

Interactive dashboards in Power BI using:

- **Matrix Tables**:  
  - Content Library  
  - User Demographics  
  - Active/Inactive Users  
  - Upgrade/Downgrade Trends  

- **Charts**:  
  - Line Charts (Time Trends)  
  - Bar/Stacked Column Charts (Category Comparisons)  
  - Donut & Pie Charts (Revenue & Distribution Analysis)

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- 🔻 **Liocinema has fewer content titles than Jotstar**  
- 📈 **Liocinema has 3x more subscribers than Jotstar**  
- 🔁 **Liocinema also leads in both upgrades and downgrades**  
- 💰 **Jotstar earns more revenue than Liocinema despite fewer users**  
- 👥 **Liocinema has a larger paid user base**  

These insights indicate Jotstar’s effective monetization strategy and Liocinema’s potential to boost content and user retention.
